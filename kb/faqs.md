# BrewBox Coffee - FAQs

## Hours and Location

Q: What are your working hours?
A: We're open Monday to Saturday, 10 AM to 7 PM IST. We're closed on Sundays and national holidays.

Q: Where are you located?
A: Our roastery and store are in Connaught Place, New Delhi. We also ship across India.

Q: Do you have a physical store I can visit?
A: Yes, the Connaught Place store is open for walk-ins during working hours. Tastings are available on Saturdays.

## Orders and Shipping

Q: How long does delivery take?
A: Metro cities get delivery in 2 to 3 business days. Other locations take 4 to 7 business days.

Q: How much is shipping?
A: Shipping is free on orders above Rs. 599. Below that, a flat Rs. 49 fee applies.

Q: Can I track my order?
A: Yes. You'll get a tracking link by SMS and email as soon as your order ships. You can also DM us your order ID and we'll check for you.

Q: Do you ship internationally?
A: Not yet. We currently deliver only within India.

Q: Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?
A: You can change or cancel within 2 hours of placing the order. After that it goes to packing and can't be modified.

## Payments

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking, and cash on delivery for orders up to Rs. 3,000.

Q: Is cash on delivery available everywhere?
A: COD is available in most serviceable pin codes. You can check yours at checkout.

## Refunds and Returns

Q: Do you offer refunds?
A: Yes, refunds are available within 7 days of delivery for unopened products.

Q: What if I receive a damaged or wrong item?
A: Sorry about that! Send us a photo of the item within 48 hours of delivery and we'll replace it or refund you, whichever you prefer.

Q: How long do refunds take?
A: Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days to your original payment method.

## Products

Q: Are your coffee beans freshly roasted?
A: Yes. Every batch is roasted to order and shipped within 48 hours of roasting.

Q: Do you offer ground coffee?
A: Yes. You can choose whole beans or a grind size (espresso, moka pot, French press, filter) at checkout.

Q: Do you have decaf options?
A: Yes, we have a Swiss Water decaf blend. It's listed under Blends.

Q: Do you offer subscriptions?
A: Yes. Subscribe and save 10 percent, with delivery every 2 or 4 weeks. You can pause or cancel anytime.

## Offers and Support

Q: Do you have any discount codes running?
A: New customers get 10 percent off their first order with the code WELCOME10. Follow our stories for flash sales.

Q: Do you offer bulk or corporate orders?
A: Yes. For orders of 5 kg or more, email corporate@brewbox.example for special pricing.

Q: How can I contact support?
A: DM us here or email support@brewbox.example. We reply within a few hours during working hours.
